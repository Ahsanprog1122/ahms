The Five Fundamental Wealth Blindspots

How AHMS converts invisible portfolio hazards into actionable metrics.

01

Fragmented Holdings

Shares in PSX brokers, gold in bank lockers, real estate in plot files, and cash deposits in savings accounts with zero unified return calculation.

• Single unified portfolio view
02

Unrecorded Real Estate Gain

Properties bought years ago sit without dated valuations. True appreciation is invisible, and portfolio net worth is perpetually underestimated.

• Dated valuations + 90-day review alerts
03

Catastrophic Concentration

Over 60% of total wealth committed to a single parcel of land or speculative stock. Risk is only discovered when the market experiences a drawdown.

• HHI score penalty for >20% single holdings
04

Illiquid Asset Lockup

Wealth is predominantly locked in illiquid property. In sudden liquidity events, investors face severe distressed discounts to raise cash.

• 50% target liquid buffer tracking
05

Hidden Decay

Holdings yielding 0% income or suffering continuous unrealized loss are concealed behind strong overall portfolio totals.

• Automatic status flags: Healthy, Watch, Weak
