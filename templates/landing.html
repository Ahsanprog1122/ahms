The Problem & Who It Helps

Five Common Gaps in Wealth Management

Traditional apps answer "what do I own?". AHMS answers "is my wealth balanced and safe?".

1

Scattered Holdings

You hold PSX shares, a residential plot, gold tolas, and bank savings in separate notebooks and apps with no single net value or return figure.

AHMS puts all physical & financial assets in one place.
2

Invisible Real Estate Growth

A plot bought for PKR 5,000,000 in 2024 is "probably worth more," but appreciation is never recorded or tracked over time.

Dated manual valuations with 90-day review reminders.
3

Concentration Risk

60% of your wealth sits in one commercial property or one high-risk stock, and danger is only felt after a catastrophic market drop.

Measures HHI & warns when one holding exceeds limits.
4

Illiquid Traps

Most wealth is tied up in illiquid land. When emergency cash is needed, owners are forced into distressed sales at discounts.

Tracks liquid share vs. 50% target to protect emergency liquidity.
5

Hidden Underperformers

A holding has lost value or generated 0% income for over a year, but goes unnoticed because total net worth still looks high.

Per-asset health classification: Healthy, Watch, or Weak.
